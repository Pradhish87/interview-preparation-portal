ANSWER STRATEGY

Structure Your Answers with STAR

A simple framework for answering behavioral questions.

S
Situation

Explain the situation or context you were dealing with.

T
Task

Describe your responsibility or objective.

A
Action

Explain the specific actions you personally took.

R
Result

Share the outcome and what you learned from the experience.

HR INTERVIEW TIP

Be Honest, Clear and Confident

Don't memorize answers word-for-word. Understand your experiences and explain them naturally with confidence.
